02 / ABOUT THE WORK

About
Red Dead Redemption

This was a blast of a project to be able to work on. I remember when I was probably around 14 playing RDR on my Xbox 360. I'd run to Fort Mercer with my friends and we'd spend all day defending the fort from other online players leading to hour-long shootouts. Being able to bring this title to modern consoles as well as the long anticipated port to PC holds a special place in my heart.

From a technical standpoint some of the most impactful work I did on this project lies in the backend. One of these is helping co-develop the D3D12 rendering backend. I was directly responsible for the buffer sub-allocation stratergy that saved us hundreds of MiB in CPU & GPU memory. I also scored us a 5-7ms/frame GPU time reduction which directly allowed some GPUs to go from mid to high quality settings.
